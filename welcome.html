💡 Tips for Success

  • Start Small: Begin with just 2-3 habits to avoid overwhelm
  • Be Consistent: Try to complete your habits at the same time each day
  • Track Progress: Checking off completed habits builds momentum
  • Be Patient: It typically takes 21-66 days to build a new habit
  • Celebrate Wins: Acknowledge your progress and reward yourself
